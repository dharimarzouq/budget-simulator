How we know

Every figure on the budget page comes from two things: the 2026-27 rules, written down with their sources below, and a made-up population of households weighted to match published UK totals. Costings are checked against HMRC's own; the method, the checks, and where they fall short are written up in full in COSTING.md and POPULATION.md in the project.
